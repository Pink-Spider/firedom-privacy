Frequently asked questions

  • Where is my data stored? — Entirely on your device. There is no server. See the privacy policy for details.
  • How do I move my data to a new phone? — In Settings, export an encrypted backup file, then import it on the new device.
  • Which currency does the app use? — You choose during onboarding, and you can change it later in Settings. Changing it relabels your amounts; it does not convert them, so pick the currency you actually record in.
  • How do I change the language? — The app follows your device language. You can also pick Korean, English or Japanese directly in Settings.
  • How do I cancel my subscription? — Subscriptions are managed by your store account. iOS: Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Android: Play Store → profile → Payments and subscriptions.
  • What happens to my data if I delete the app? — It is deleted along with the app. Export a backup file first if you want to keep it.
